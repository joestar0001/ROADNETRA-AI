The 'Vision in the Wild' Challenge

Why Single-Pass Vision Fails in the Wild

Operating on real CCTV footage means dealing with blinding night glare, rain streaks, pole vibrations, and perspective distortions. Standard models pushed to high sensitivity routinely hallucinate: mistaking silver cars for highway dividers and manhole covers for dangerous craters.

The ROADNETRA Innovation

Cascaded 'Propose & Judge' Architecture

ROADNETRA runs a two-stage cascaded pipeline: Stage 1 operates on the full wide-angle frame to maximize Recall, while our dedicated Stage-2 Judge Model inspects zoomed-in crops with 25% contextual padding to veto moving vehicle false alarms before dispatching tickets.
