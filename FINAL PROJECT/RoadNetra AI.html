Operational Robustness ('Vision in the Wild')

☀️ / 🌙

Night Glare & Illumination

Trained with heavy HSV saturation & brightness jitter (hsv_v=0.4, hsv_s=0.7) to withstand blinding oncoming vehicle high-beams and dark unlit highway stretches.

💨 / 🌧️

Camera Sway & Motion Blur

Injected synthetic Gaussian & motion blur with rotational jitter (degrees=10.0) to remain resilient when roadside poles sway in heavy monsoons and high winds.

🔍 / 🚗

Occlusion & Moving Vehicles

Full mosaic augmentations (mosaic=1.0) enable extraction of road defects partially hidden behind trucks, while Stage-2 Judge vetoes vehicle reflections.
